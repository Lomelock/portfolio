About Me

I am a passionate and versatile professional with a strong background in web development, design and tourism. I hold a Diploma in Records Management and a Tour Operator Certificate, and I am fluent in English and Swahili. With expertise in Wix, WordPress and HTML, I build responsive websites for personal, business and e-commerce clients.

I also create marketing materials such as brochures, business cards and tour packages in Canva, and I have hands-on experience in email and website management. Whether I'm working with local businesses or international clients, I bring a detail-oriented approach, creative solutions and clear communication to every project.

Maasai Boma Homestay – Lake Natron

HomestayWixCultural Tourism

A website for a Maasai family homestay in Engare Sero, at the foot of Ol Doinyo Lengai. It presents authentic cultural experiences such as morning milking, cooking with Maasai mamas, chai, cattle herding walks, storytelling by the fire and traditional dance, plus an optional trip to the east side of the lake.
